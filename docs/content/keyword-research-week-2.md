## Executive Summary

This week's research focuses on three strategic areas:
1. **Long-tail buying-intent keywords** with strong CPS/affiliate revenue potential (dash cams, cold air intakes, jump starters).
2. **High-volume "how to" queries** where forum/Reddit content currently dominates page 1 -- structured long-form guides will outrank them.
3. **Category expansion** into detailing and mods/tuning, which are currently unaddressed on the site.

The top 5 picks are projected to drive an estimated 40,000--55,000 combined monthly visits once ranking in positions 1--3.

## Top 5 Priority Picks (Ranked)

### 1. "how to change your oil at home" (and variants)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Target keyword** | how to change your oil at home |
| **Est. monthly volume** | 18,000 -- 25,000 |
| **Search intent** | Informational (DIY how-to) |
| **Content type** | Step-by-step guide with tool checklist |
| **Suggested title** | *How to Change Your Oil at Home: A Step-by-Step Guide for Beginners* |
| **Category** | `diy-guides` |
| **Difficulty** | Medium |
| **Affiliate potential** | High -- oil, filters, drain pans, ramps, Fumoto valves |

**Why it wins:** This is the single highest-volume DIY automotive query. Current SERPs are dominated by YouTube carousels and thin forum threads. A well-structured guide with an embedded tools checklist, torque specs table, and vehicle-specific notes will rank. The affiliate basket is deep: oil brands, filter brands, drain pans, ramps/jack stands, and Fumoto valves all convert well.

**Content outline recommendations:**
- Safety/disclaimer section (required for YMYL signals)
- Tools and materials checklist (affiliate links inline)
- Step-by-step with photos per step
- Oil viscosity chart by vehicle make
- Disposal instructions (legal requirement in US/EU)
- FAQ (How often? Synthetic vs conventional? Can I use any filter?)

**Secondary keyword clusters to target:**
- "oil change cost vs diy"  
- "best oil for high mileage cars"
- "synthetic vs conventional oil"
- "how often should you change your oil"

## Methodology Notes

- Search volumes are estimated based on keyword planner benchmarks, competitor analysis, and industry knowledge. Actual volumes should be validated via Ahrefs/Semrush before production.
- Difficulty ratings are relative to a new site with moderate domain authority -- these are attainable targets, not head terms.
- Affiliate potential ratings factor in: average order value, conversion rate likelihood, and Amazon Associates / CJ affiliate program availability.
- Forum outrank potential assessed by manually reviewing page 1 SERPs for each target query and noting where Reddit, Quora, and niche forums currently occupy top-10 positions.
